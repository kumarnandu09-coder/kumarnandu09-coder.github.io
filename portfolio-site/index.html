About Me

I design systems and strategies, not just screens.

I combine user-centered thinking, business strategy and deep technical collaboration to build digital products that scale — from early discovery through to measured outcomes after launch. Day to day I work shoulder-to-shoulder with Product Managers, Engineers, QA, Business teams, Data teams and Leadership — translating ambiguous goals into journeys, architecture and interfaces that teams can actually ship, measure and iterate on.

AI-Native Practice

Designing with AI,
and designing for it.

AI is part of how I work and what I build. I use it to get from ambiguity to a tested prototype faster, and I design AI experiences people can trust, with clear limits, human control and measurable outcomes.

Designing AI Experiences

Conversational and agentic UX: intent preview, confidence signals, human override, undo and escalation paths.

AI-Accelerated Workflow

Research synthesis, flow mapping and UX copy drafts with Claude and ChatGPT. I own the judgment on what ships.

Prompt-to-Prototype

Real, clickable flows in Lovable and Google Stitch, tested with users in days instead of sprints.

AI-Ready Design Systems

Tokens and components structured so AI tools and code agents generate on-brand, accessible UI.

Measuring AI Quality

Success metrics for AI features: resolution rate, deflection without dead ends, fallback and trust signals.

Leading AI Adoption

Shared prompt libraries, review guardrails and playbooks so a whole team uses AI well, with human quality bars.
